How it works

  1. Filming: the capture station turns the table and records (under a minute).
  2. Picking frames: it keeps the sharpest frames, then this station copies them.
  3. Building the model: masks, camera positions, then NeRF training (about 21 minutes for 30,000 iterations).
  4. Done: preview videos appear here. The full results stay on the station until you fetch them.

The turntable films one scan at a time. If scans are waiting, make sure the right object is on the table when its turn comes.
